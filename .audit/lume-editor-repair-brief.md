Remove denied resource tabs, clear its scoped draft, selection and next-send context, hide cached content, and allow navigation away. Do not require clicking the current tab to trigger it. Keep the controller's navigation generation and existing result guards intact. Private draft behavior remains unchanged apart from protecting async replacement.
