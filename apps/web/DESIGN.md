# Lume design system

Ink, paper and one grey, laid out on a grid of hard lines. The interface is plain enough to trust and sharp enough to remember: large, tightly tracked type, mono labels in capitals, square corners, and an earthy orange that arrives on hover and marks what the Lume did. The reference is editorial and Swiss rather than soft SaaS; it stays calm because everything lines up.

The UI is built on [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com) (Radix, `radix-nova` preset) with Tailwind v4. Primitives live in `src/components/ui/` and are ours to edit. The palette, radius and easing are set on the shadcn tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, so every primitive inherits them. Style with Tailwind utilities, and don't hardcode hex values.

## Authenticated workspace

The office follows the floating-panel prototype supplied on 6 October 2026. These rules supersede the square grid, colorful module icons and sidebar rules for authenticated screens. The public landing and authentication layouts retain their existing composition and use the current Lume logo.

`src/components/lume/lume-workspace.tsx` owns one persistent private conversation under `(office)/layout.tsx`. Existing App Router children form the canvas. The desktop panel is `clamp(360px, 33.333%, 500px)` wide, inset 10px, with a 16px radius, a restrained shadow and a warm panel. Focused and collapsed modes retain the mounted conversation. The collapsed launcher uses the real Lume mark on a subtle peach circle. It does not imply a running task.

`src/app/lume-workspace.css` maps semantic tokens inside the workspace. Canvas is `#FDFDFB`, panel `#FAFAF8`, ink `#1C1B19`, accent `#E08A6B`. Borders use ink at 9%, inputs at 20%, secondary surfaces `#EFEEEA`. Chrome is monochrome. Rounded tabs, menus and the composer use 6–16px radii. The next-message context chip identifies the actual authorized resource and explicit sources. A running request names its original target when the canvas changes. Never invent planned stages or progress.

Dark workspace tokens use canvas `#1A1918`, panel `#1E1D1B`, raised surface `#222120`, text `#F3F1EC` and restrained white borders. Light is the default. A saved explicit theme still wins.

The launcher derives its destinations from `navigation.ts`, including feature flags and platform administration. The account menu retains profile, tutorial, feedback, installation, theme and sign-out. The notification count and dialog remain accessible in the toolbar. A failed save prevents explicit tab or link navigation; sign-out offers the existing explicit discard choice after a save fails.

On mobile, a fresh root visit starts in chat. Explicit case and document links start in the canvas. Lume and Canvas controls alternate two mounted surfaces. The hidden surface is inert. Controls have at least 44px touch targets. Layout uses `dvh`, safe-area insets, keyboard focus and reduced-motion preferences. No horizontal page overflow.

Tabs contain canonical resource URLs and IDs. They prefetch their routes and update the selected tab immediately. The `(office)/loading.tsx` boundary keeps route loading inside the canvas so the shell and chat remain mounted. Stored tab URLs are scoped to the user and office; titles are obtained only after authorization. Navigation changes the next message context. Each sent message keeps a copied context, and regeneration uses its server-stored scope with fresh authorization. An unresolved destination disables sending. Private conversation and artifact ownership do not change.

History, new conversation, expand and collapse share one row in the Lume header. Personalizar Lume lives in Meu perfil at `/app/profile/lume`. Artefatos belongs to each case in the Cofre. Account dialogs, including notifications, feedback and the tutorial welcome, are centered on desktop and mobile. Portaled controls inherit the workspace palette and radii from the body while the office shell is mounted.

## Public screen principles

1. **The grid is the decoration.** Structure comes from 1px `line` rules (ink in light, grey in dark) that split the shell, headers and sections into cells. Cells meet edge to edge; nothing floats on a margin except overlays.
2. **Ink first, color on contact.** Hierarchy comes from size, tracking and the ink ramp (`foreground`, `muted-foreground`, `subtle-foreground`). `primary` is ink. `brand` (earthy orange) appears when something is touched (hover fills, focus) and marks the Lume's own work. Each module keeps one hue on its icon and on 2px markers.
3. **Square.** `--radius` is 0. Only dots and avatars are round.
4. **Say it once.** A page gets one title. A mono label may name a section; don't add subtitles that repeat the title.
5. **Real states, plain words.** Empty, loading, and error states are short sentences in Portuguese, set in text color. No illustrations, no colored alert boxes.

## Banned patterns

- Badges, pills, and chips used to decorate or show status. Show status as plain text.
  Exception: the requested `BETA` label for Anúncios uses small mono type, a square outline in current text color, and no fill. Show it beside the module name in navigation and the page header, including mobile.
- Pastel or tinted background fills. Exceptions: `brand-soft` on the selected conversation; flat `panel` grey and solid `brand` blocks on the landing.
- Bullet-point lists in the app UI. Use a table, rows, or a sentence. The landing may list features as mono lines led by a brand square.
- Containers inside containers, meaning a bordered card inside a bordered panel.
- On public screens, rounded corners, gradients, glows, emoji and decorative icons next to headings. The office workspace uses the rounded system above. The supplied Reflexo animation accompanies smart options and actual Lume work.

## Dark theme and installation

Light is the default theme. When dark is selected, public-screen paper becomes `#232323`, ink becomes `#f5f5f5`, and the grid lines soften to grey so the page doesn't turn into a cage. Primary controls invert to light ink; brand fills keep ink text. Keep using semantic tokens; don't add per-component color overrides.

`ThemeSwitch` (`src/components/theme-provider.tsx`) is two small circles, one hollow and one filled; the filled one slides to the side of the current theme, and a click flips it. It has an accessible label naming the theme a click leads to. It lives in the workspace account menu, the auth header and the landing header. `InstallApp` shares the same quiet controls. Installation help is a dialog; offline and pending-update notices use a single floating surface and plain pt-BR text.

## Type

- **Sans**: Geist, everywhere (`--font-geist`), tracked slightly tight (-0.011em body, -0.03em headings). 14px body, 13px meta, 12px field errors. Weights 400, 450 for display, 500 for emphasis. `font-serif` and `font-heading` resolve to the same face.
- **Mono**: Geist Mono (`--font-geist-mono`) through the `label-mono` utility: 12px, capitals, +0.02em. Use it for section labels (led by a `square-dot`), counts, dates in meta rows and the text of arrow actions. Never for sentences.
- **Display**: `display` sets 450 weight, -0.045em tracking and 0.95 line height; the size is chosen per use (auth heading 44px, 64px from `md`; the landing up to 280px). Page titles use `page-title` (30px, 38px from `md`, -0.04em).
- **Page spacing**: existing canvas modules keep their content spacing (`px-5 py-6`, `md:px-10 md:py-10`) and reading widths. The persistent canvas toolbar owns navigation. Do not align new module headers to a removed sidebar.

## Logo

Its alpha mask comes from the unchanged `public/lume-mark.png` and inherits `currentColor`. `<Logo height={n} />` pairs it with the Geist nameplate. `public/lume.svg`, `src/app/icon.svg` and the offline page contain the same image mask. `scripts/generate-pwa-icons.ts` uses that PNG to generate the favicon and PWA assets. Halftone fields use the same silhouette. Don't put the logo inside page content.

### Reflexo

`public/lume-reflexo.gif` is the supplied minimalist animation. Smart options show it on hover or keyboard focus, and SmartWorking shows it during an actual request. The static PNG remains visible under reduced motion. Light and dark workspace styles blend the GIF's background into the surface.

## Halftone

`<Halftone />` (`src/components/halftone.tsx`) is the product's one image: square ink pixels, ordered-dithered over slow noise, with a sprinkle of brand pixels in the mid-tones and, optionally, the Lume mark cut out of it (`markTone`). Where text sits over the field (the landing) the mark is a flat `panel` silhouette in the field's own pixels. Where it stands alone (sign-in and sign-up) it is a `brand` symbol over a paper cut-out one cell wider than the mark, so its supplied silhouette stays sharp. It leans toward the pointer. It is decorative (`aria-hidden`), pauses off screen and in hidden tabs, draws one still frame under reduced motion, re-reads the palette when the theme changes, and caps itself near 60k pixels per frame by growing its cells on large areas. It fills the sign-in panel and the landing's image cells; it never goes inside the app's working screens.

## Components

Prefer a shadcn primitive over new markup. Add one with `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add <name>`.

- `Button`: `default` is ink, and the brand sweeps in from the left on hover (`hover-sweep`) while the label turns ink; `outline` is drawn in ink and fills with ink from the bottom (`hover-rise`); `ghost` takes a quiet `accent` fill; `destructive` is outlined in red and fills red. One primary action per view. A leading action may put its arrow at the far end (`justify-between`), as on the auth screens.
- `Input`, `Label`, `Textarea` in a `grid gap-1.5` field, with the error as a `text-destructive text-xs` line below. Form-level errors are an inline `CircleAlert` plus red text, never a filled box. A form that needs a choice checks it itself (`noValidate`) and says what is missing under the field, never in the browser's bubble.
- **Selects** look the same wherever they are: a native `select`, the `SelectTrigger` and the trigger of a searchable picker (`PickerTrigger`, `src/components/ui/picker-trigger.tsx`) are outlined in `input`, start their text 10px in and draw the lucide chevron in `muted-foreground` 10px from the right edge. For native selects this is one unlayered rule in `globals.css` (so a component's `px-*` can't move the text or the arrow); a select inside a sentence opts out of the box with `data-inline`. When a choice needs searching, use one picker (the trigger opens a search with the matches under it), never a search field beside a select.
- **Section tabs** (Escritório, Pesquisa, Honorários, Integrações, Administração) share `sectionTab` (`src/components/section-tabs.ts`): the text at the page margin, 48px rows from `md` (44px on a phone), the active tab in medium weight over a 2px ink rule, the others in `muted-foreground`, all under one hairline. Three or more tabs become a 3-column grid on a phone. The document workspace's tabs keep their `brand` rule: they belong to the Lume's own work.
- **Form dialogs** keep the fields in a scrolling area between the title and a fixed row of actions under a `line`, so Cancelar and Salvar stay in view on a phone. Their overlay dims like every other (`bg-overlay/40`, the tab bar included).
- `Sheet` (`side="bottom"`) remains available for contextual module actions. The module launcher and account utilities use the persistent shell menus.
- **Workspace menu** lives beside the canvas tabs. Module icons stay monochrome; flagged modules and Administração appear only for eligible users. The account menu holds utility actions. See the authenticated workspace rules above.
- **App shell** is the persistent `(office)/layout.tsx` and `src/components/lume/`. The shell owns panel modes and canvas tabs. App Router owns route content and active URLs.
- **Mobile switch** offers Lume and Canvas in a safe-area-aware bottom bar. It never unmounts either surface. Module navigation is available through Abrir módulos in the canvas toolbar.
- **Bottom sheets** float: inset 8px from the side edges, above the tab bar, square and outlined with a `line`. That geometry is plain CSS in `globals.css` (`[data-slot="sheet-content"][data-side="bottom"]`), unlayered so it beats the utility classes, with a static `bottom` fallback before the `env()` one.
- **Overlays dim, they don't blur.** `backdrop-filter` breaks stacking in some Safari versions, which can hide the panel behind the blur layer. A flat `bg-overlay/40` is also cheaper on a phone and stays dark in both themes. The feedback panel is the one exception: it blurs the page lightly (`backdrop-blur-[6px]` over `overlay/20`), and its panel is a later sibling of the overlay in the same portal, so it paints above the blur layer.
- **Agent chat** stays mounted in the office layout. Its conversation history overlays the panel when requested. The composer stays at the bottom of the scrollable thread, uses a 14px radius and displays the context of the next message. Streaming and approval states come from the existing runtime. Only an explicit conversation change replaces that runtime. Loading, empty and error messages remain plain pt-BR.
- **Composer controls** live inside the composer, on a row under the input: attach and microphone. While recording, the microphone becomes "Descartar gravação" (trash), a rolling level meter in `brand` bars and a timer fill the row, and the send button becomes a stop square that finishes the recording. The audio is transcribed ("Transcrevendo áudio…"), joined to anything typed and sent as the person's own message, so the thread shows the words, never "Mensagem de voz". Under reduced motion the meter stays still and the timer carries the state. The page header keeps only the conversation actions. The model is chosen by the platform administrator for the office and is never shown in the chat. A control the configured model cannot support stays visible and disabled, with a plain tooltip explaining the limitation.
- **Case artifacts** (`src/components/case-artifacts.tsx`) live under Artefatos in each Cofre case. List only the person's related private documents and authorized saved copies. Original documents open in the canvas; saved copies open as Vault files. Salvar no Cofre uses an inline form with the case selected, format and folder choice, retry and a persisted success status. Do Cofre nesta conversa chooses authorized case sources for the next private message.
- **Chat attachments** stay in the message, independently from the Cofre. The rounded attach menu offers camera capture, existing images, or documents; the pickers take several files at once (up to six per message, documents up to 25 MB, images up to 10 MB), and the ones that cannot be attached are named in one error line. A document brings up to 300 thousand characters of text; a Word file also brings its pictures (up to 24, in reading order), so a guide made of pasted screenshots is read like the screenshots themselves. When a conversation holds more attachment text than one turn can carry, the newest files are read in full and the oldest are left out with a note to the model, never an error. Show removable previews above the composer before sending and persistent previews in the user message afterward. Request camera access only after "Tirar foto", allow retaking before attaching, and stop the camera when the dialog closes.
- **Opções inteligentes** (`src/components/smart-options.tsx`) show the current Lume symbol and the supplied Reflexo GIF on hover, focus or actual work. The menu uses the workspace radius, popover surface and quiet action rows. Enter/Space/Down opens it; arrows move between options and Escape closes it. Reduced motion keeps the static symbol.
- **Documents open in the canvas** at `/app/documents/[id]`. Legacy `?doc=` links redirect there. The private editor retains Editar, Página and Revisão, template typography, export, version history, autosave and conflict recovery. The shared `DocumentDraftsProvider` keeps unsaved work across routes and mobile switches. Selecting text exposes Pedir ao Lume, saves the document first and sends a copied selection to the persistent chat. Real tool writes invalidate the corresponding editor. A delayed output can add a background tab but cannot replace a resource the person opened after sending.
- **The Lume acts, then says what it did.** Observed tool calls appear as quiet module-labelled rows above the answer. A running row updates by the same call ID when the result arrives; only a confirmed result gets a check and an "Abrir" link when available. Pending approval, cancellation, failure and interruption say precisely what happened. There are no invented stages, totals or assistant personas. Destructive actions, external sends and draft overwrites retain their existing confirmation gates: a sentence with a 2px `brand` rule and Confirmar/Cancelar buttons; the decision replaces the buttons with plain text.

Right, behind a `line`: the `Halftone` with the Lume mark. On mobile the field is a 144px strip between the header and the form. The field answers the form: it tightens and drifts faster while the password has focus, flashes darker on submit and stills and pales on an error. The cell carries no text.

Left: a mono "Plano Lume" label, the state as a `display` sentence ("Ativo até 26 de setembro de 2026", "Venceu em …", "Sem plano ativo") and the price in one line. In an invite form, a valid address that has an account shows a line under the field (avatar, name as the trigger, "já usa o Lume", atuação); one without says the invite becomes a link. ## Landing

`/` (`src/app/page.tsx`) is the public page; signed-out visitors land here and its actions lead to `/sign-in` and `/sign-up`. It is the identity at full volume and stays short: header, hero, one statement band, modules, three figures, a call to action and the footer. Copy is pt-BR and says only what the product does today; never invent numbers or clients.

- **Header**: sticky, two cells under a `line`: the mark tile and the São Paulo clock; then anchor links (`hover-rise`), the theme toggle and an ink "Entrar" block with an arrow (`hover-sweep`). On mobile: tile, name and "Entrar".
- **Grid**: every section is a row of cells split by `line` rules. Cells are paper, ink (`bg-foreground text-background`), `panel` grey, solid `brand` with ink text, or a `Halftone`. Big words use `display`, often in capitals, sized with `clamp()` so they fill their cell on any width.
- **Modules** list on ink: number in mono, name at display size, a line figure, a short paragraph and four mono lines led by brand squares.
- **Call to action**: words set on ink and paper blocks over a full-width `Halftone`, ending in a brand arrow tile; the whole block is one link.

## Motion

Motion is smooth and slow to settle: everything eases with `--ease` (expo out), so it starts at once and lands softly. It explains what changed or answers a touch; it never loops inside the app. The exception is the Opções inteligentes mark, which loops only while hovered or while the Lume is working (the smart options, e-mail summaries and the chat's working line), and stays still under reduced motion. Everything goes through `gsap.matchMedia`, a `prefers-reduced-motion` check or the global reduced-motion rule in `globals.css`, so reduced-motion users get instant changes. Radix components animate through `tw-animate-css` instead, and that's fine.

- **Hover fills** are the signature. `hover-rise` raises a block of color from the bottom in 0.6s (nav rows, header links); `hover-sweep` sweeps it in from the left in 0.8s (primary buttons, arrow links, the mark tile). The fill is `--fill`, `brand` by default; text colors cross-fade over 0.5–0.7s so they land with the fill. Both also show on `:focus-visible`, and only on devices that hover.
- **Arrows** slide 4px toward where they lead while their action is hovered.
- **Entrance**: wrap a page in `<Reveal>` and mark children with `data-reveal`. They fade and lift in order (0.35s, 40ms stagger). Use it once per page load, and never on scroll-heavy lists.
- **Selection**: canvas tabs use a quiet neutral active background. Keyboard focus remains visible. Panel modes and mobile surface switches preserve mounted content.
- **Chrome**: the mobile Lume/Canvas switch stays reachable while content scrolls. Do not animate it out of view.
- **Micro**: other hover and press states are Tailwind transitions of 150–300ms with `ease-(--ease)`. Don't use GSAP for these.
- **Landing only** (`src/components/landing/`): the hero title and copy are visible at first paint. Below the viewport, words rise out of their line box (`data-rise`), blocks fade and lift (`data-fade`), and color tiles uncover from the bottom (`data-wipe`). IntersectionObserver starts native Web Animations once per element, using `--ease`; reduced motion and keyboard focus cancel active effects. A strip of module names scrolls slowly and pauses on hover, and line figures turn a quarter while their row is hovered.
- The `Halftone` is the only continuous motion outside the landing strip. It is decorative, paused when hidden or off screen, and still under reduced motion.

## Mobile

- The viewport is `viewport-fit=cover` with `interactive-widget=resizes-content` (set in `src/app/layout.tsx`), so the keyboard resizes the layout and the docked composer stays visible.
- The active canvas tab names its module. Existing module headings may remain visually hidden on mobile; titles naming a case, client, document or judgment stay visible.
- Respect the safe areas in the shell top inset and the bottom Lume/Canvas switch. Dialogs must clear the switch.
- Use `dvh`, not `vh`.
- Nothing widens the page: grids of cards use `grid-cols-1` (a track of min-width 0) so a long name ends in an ellipsis inside its card, and toolbars wrap with `min-w-0`.
- A page's actions sit on the right of its header, the title being hidden there; the main create action carries its `Plus`.
- Section tabs with more views than fit (Escritório, Administração) show all of them in a 3-column grid on phones, never a strip that scrolls and cuts a word.

## Future patterns

- **Lists and tables**: full-width rows separated by a hairline, a 13px `muted-foreground` header row, and no zebra striping. Show a file or category with a small icon and text, not a pill.
- **Menus and popovers**: authenticated shell menus use the warm semantic surface, a subtle border, 12px corners and a quiet shadow.

- **The private chat greeting** uses the actual first name, local time/date and selected context. Suggested prompts prefill the composer only after a click. They make no claims about work that has not been loaded. Changing the canvas or switching mobile surfaces keeps the conversation and its draft.
- **Calendar** pins count all matching activities in the visible month, including paginated results. Tasks use civil dates; meetings mark every overlapping local day, excluding their ending midnight. Pin counts are included in accessible labels.
- **Client details** live at `/app/agenda/clients/[id]`, with contact, address, notes, linked cases and paginated activities. Practice areas appear as plain text next to the relationship ("Cliente ativo · Cível, Trabalhista"), never as chips. A dialog is used only to edit data. The Agenda navigation stays active on nested client routes.
- **Done work leaves the list.** A module's main list shows what still needs attention; finished items move to an archive tab in the same module (Notificações: Novas / Arquivadas; Tarefas: Abertas / Arquivadas, filtered by Concluídas or Canceladas). Reopening an item returns it to the main list.
- **Notificações** is a floating panel, not a section: the Bell in the canvas toolbar opens it in the same place as the feedback panel, over a dimmed page. Title, then two tabs (Novas, Arquivadas) and "Arquivar todas"; rows are the title, one clamped line of summary and a mono time, with an archive icon on new ones. Opening or archiving a notification moves it to Arquivadas. Preferences and push devices are not in the panel (`src/components/notification-settings.tsx` waits for a settings page). `/app/notifications` and `?notificacoes=1` open the panel. The same Bell shows the unread count on desktop and mobile.
- **Pesquisa** (`/app/research`) has two modality tabs: Marcas and Jurisprudência. Use `sectionTabRow` so both fit on phones. Each modality offers Pesquisar and Histórico. Marcas starts with name/logo square choices, an image preview and labeled territory/status/Nice/matching selects; Brazil and all statuses are defaults. Results are rows with representation, name linking to internal details, holder, status and the WIPO source. New name and logo queries use the WIPO Global Brand Database browser for every territory, including Brazil. Phonetic matching is available for names. Details show the source, collection time and published fields, including official status and origin-office link. Loading, partial results and blocked queries are explicit; no match never certifies availability. Jurisprudência uses the existing corpus and permitted official sources. Histories remain private to the person. New deep links use `mode` and `search`; legacy Web links open a new Marcas search. Public web search remains available to the agent through `web_search`.
- **Feedback** is a floating panel, not a section: the Bug icon in the account menu opens it centered in the viewport, over a softly blurred page (on mobile, above the Lume/Canvas switch). Title and one line; three rounded tiles with an icon (Algo quebrou, Tenho uma ideia, Não entendi algo; the chosen one is ink); one textarea whose question follows the tile; a bordered row saying what goes with it (the screen, as an inline native select preset from where the person is, and a reminder not to include client data); a bordered row to include a screenshot (picked or pasted, with a thumbnail); a full-width primary action named for the tile (Reportar problema, Enviar ideia, Enviar dúvida); then "Ver seus relatos", which switches the panel to the person's reports as rows with plain-text status and the team's answer. `?feedback=relatos` (used by the resolution notification and the old `/app/feedback`) opens it there. Only say what is actually sent.
- **Administração** (`/app/admin`) is a module of the shell for platform administrators: the `page-title` "Administração", then the section tabs (Feedback, Clientes, Financeiro, IA, Execuções, Credenciais, Auditoria). **IA** holds everything the platform's AI needs, once for every office: the Lume (its models per task, then the provider connections) and TypeSafe, as two sections with `text-2xl` headings split by a `line`. **Modelos por tarefa** is a list of groups as rows (name and one line on the left, the resolved "conexão · modelo · esforço" with where each comes from in plain text, then Editar and Testar); each group's tasks follow as indented rows that read "Segue o grupo." until they get a choice of their own. Editing opens the form under the row and says which tasks the change reaches and which keep their own choice. A broken choice (its connection disabled or deleted) is a red line with the reason, never a silent fallback. Each connection row says what it serves, and unchecking "Conexão ativa" on one in use names what will stop before saving. **Clientes** is a plain list of offices (name, people, since). A record inside a tab (a ticket) uses a `text-2xl` heading under a back link. **Auditoria** lists `platform_audit_log` newest first: filters as outlined links (Tudo, Créditos, Pagamentos, IA, TypeSafe, WhatsApp, Credenciais), then rows with the action in medium weight, the person and the office (a link that narrows the list to that office) on a 13px line, the details as plain text, and the time in mono on the right; "Mais antigos →" loads the next page. The feedback queue is a filtered list of rows ordered by priority, then impact; triage signals (security, personal data, low confidence) are plain text, and the model's suggestion is shown with its confidence next to the editable fields.
- **E-mails** (`/app/email`, `src/components/google/gmail-panel.tsx`) is laid out like a mail client, edge to edge under the page title: a folder column (Escrever as the primary action beside the Opções inteligentes mark, then Recebidos, Com estrela, Enviados, Rascunhos and Todos as 40px rows with icons, `hover-rise`, and the ink block on the active one), the conversation list (search on top; sender, time, subject and snippet per row; the open one keeps an ink rule on its left) and the reading pane. The folder column shows labels from `xl` and becomes an icon rail with tooltips below it; on phones the folders are a horizontal strip, Escrever and the mark sit in the header, and the list and the reading pane take turns. A message's HTML is shown as its sender designed it in a sandboxed frame (`src/components/google/email-frame.tsx`: no scripts, no forms, links in a new tab, a white page in both themes), with remote images blocked until "Mostrar imagens"; messages without HTML are plain text. The mailbox's smart options are "Resumo do dia / da semana / do mês" and "Classificar esta página"; the summary opens in the reading pane with Dia, Semana and Mês as square options, a headline under the `brand` rule, "Pedem atenção" rows and one short paragraph per subject with its conversations as rows, closed by one line saying what was read. Inside a conversation the mark offers "Resumo e respostas rápidas": the overview and the points that matter above the messages, then up to three replies as rows that open the reply editor filled in — nothing is sent without the person. Reviewers get the overview without replies.

## Associates and folder access

Each lawyer has one personal office. The shell has no office switcher or team tab. Platform administration stays separate.

Escritório uses Associados, Convites and Atividade. Atividade reads every audit log of the office newest first, as sentences after the actor's name ("Ana, pelo Lume, enviou um e-mail", "Rotina automática consultou o tribunal"), with the source and the outcome in words on a 13px line under each, outlined filter links (Tudo, Processos, Acessos, Google, Anúncios, Buscas) and "Mais antigos →" for the next page. Case Participantes lists the owner and collaborators; the owner includes people from their associates. Empty, loading and error states use plain pt-BR text and retry controls. Removal dialogs explain revocation and preservation of existing files.

Folder creation offers Quem vê a pasta: Todos do caso, Só eu, or Pessoas escolhidas. Restricted access uses labeled checkboxes for case members and loading, empty and error states. Only the creator gets Acesso à pasta. Visibility is plain text on rows; private folders are absent for other viewers. Ancestors restrict descendants.

Personalizar Lume, reached from Meu perfil at `/app/profile/lume`, shows one list of rules, one list of knowledge and one effective Word template. Legacy settings remain editable. Controls preserve 44px touch targets, labels, keyboard focus and scrollable dialogs.
